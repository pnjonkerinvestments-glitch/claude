# Roviko 1.7 — game collection and curious clues

Mosaic’s former region hints allowed players to solve boards through continent elimination. New four- and five-clue boards draw from 80 bilingual clues about 40 verified natural and cultural sites: fossils, wildlife, caves, landscapes, water and heritage. A seeded variant changes the clue, and another country from the same continent is included when available. Correct matches unlock a short explanation with source credit; incorrect clues retain the existing immediate red feedback. All matching, retries, saved progress and hints remain supported.

The homepage now presents three original, tactile illustrated covers over clean, consistently structured game cards. Manrope, cool neutral surfaces, blue controls, restrained borders and consistent spacing replace the previous mix of softer fonts and card treatments. Daily progress, weekly play history, tomorrow’s topic, practice options, regional quick games, rooms and the passport retain their existing data and actions. Small screens use horizontal cards, with a stacked fallback below 360px. Both themes and reduced-motion behaviour are retained.

There is no migration or data reset. Existing sessions and frozen daily snapshots are deliberately not rewritten. New practice boards receive the new clue catalogue immediately; future daily snapshots receive it at creation. The catalogue and exact source/license records are downloadable from the source page. No competitor assets or copy were imported.

Verification and remaining limits are recorded in `QA_1_7.md`.

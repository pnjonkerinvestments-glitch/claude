# Notes for App Review (App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes)

Copy the text below into the "Notes" field. It is in English because the reviewer reads English.

---

Roviko is a daily geography game: every day there is a 20-question "Daily Detour" and five short puzzle games (Rank Radar, World Duel, Side by Side, Country Mosaic, Clue Trail) with points and a daily ranking, plus classic flag, capital and map games. It is free and has no in-app purchases.

**No login is needed.** The app creates a guest profile automatically. You can play everything as a guest. An account (email + password) is optional and only needed for the friends list.

**Testing multiplayer with one device:** open the "Friends" tab, then "Play against the computer", pick a level (Easy, Medium or Hard) and tap "Play now". A full match starts against a computer opponent. "Play against a random player" pairs two real players who are searching at the same time; if nobody is found within 3 minutes, the app offers to play against the computer instead.

**User-generated content (guideline 1.2):** the only user text other players see is the display name. There is no chat.
- Names are filtered on the server (English, Dutch and Spanish, including leetspeak and spacing tricks).
- Every other player has a "⋯" button (in the room lobby, the end-of-match ranking and the friends list) to **report** the player (four fixed reasons) or **block** them. Blocked players are never matched or put in the same room again.
- Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. The terms (roviko.app/terms) forbid offensive names and cheating.

**Privacy, contact and account deletion:** Passport tab (bottom right) → "Account and privacy":
- Contact (support@roviko.app)
- Privacy policy
- Download my data
- Delete my account (works for guests too; deletes all data immediately)

The privacy policy is also at https://roviko.app/privacy, the terms at https://roviko.app/terms.

**App-specific features:** optional daily reminder (local notification, on the "All games" page in the Play tab), an offline screen when there is no connection, app layout without browser elements, and a fixed game controls bar at the bottom of the screen.

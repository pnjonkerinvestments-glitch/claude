# Brand and originality review

Working title: **Roviko**, configured centrally in `lib/config.ts`.

The product treats geography as a friendly rivalry: an open play shelf, a prominent room card, a small daily itinerary and a personal passport. The visual thesis is a night-sky play space with warm orange actions, lavender social accents, six distinct mode tones, a tactile globe illustration and clear typographic hierarchy. The light theme uses crisp white surfaces with the same semantic accents. The homepage is a game selection surface; it does not put a long marketing story before play.

Ten candidates for later brand selection (proposals, not availability claims):

1. Roviko — a compact, friendly rover-inspired working title.
2. Latilo — latitude with an approachable ending.
3. Orvato — orbit and voyage associations.
4. Merivo — meridian and movement.
5. Wandri — wandering and discovery.
6. Teravo — earth and forward motion.
7. Norilo — direction and a light international sound.
8. Atlino — atlas-inspired, without a geo/trivia prefix.
9. Voyora — voyage and a social call to play.
10. Rovora — roaming with a round, playful sound.

Review performed against the requested originality constraints:

- No GeoTrivia name variation, logo, screenshots, assets, UI copy, question collection or code was imported.
- The orange R-and-pin mark was authored as an SVG for this app. The globe is a new decorative generation.
- Navigation uses ordinary functional labels requested by the brief. The layout is an independent game shelf plus invitation panel, not a reconstruction of a competitor screenshot.
- Clue Trail, City Circuit, Flag Signal, Pinpoint, Next Door and Size Shuffle are independently selected descriptive working names. General words may occur elsewhere; this is not a uniqueness guarantee or legal opinion.
- All questions are generated from attributed open country facts. Flag and map licences are shipped.
- Dark navy/orange/lavender is a common palette combination used here with original component styling and typography. No competitor stylesheet was consulted.

A preliminary category search did not establish a known geography quiz product called Roviko, but it did find unrelated uses of the word. The working name is therefore **not trademark-cleared**. Conduct proper name/domain/trademark checks before a commercial brand commitment.

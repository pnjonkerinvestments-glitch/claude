# Captions, pinned comments and hashtags

Post as a photo carousel:
- **TikTok:** the 12 slides from `out/post-N/tiktok/` (3:4, fills TikTok's photo frame).
- **Instagram:** the 12 slides from `out/post-N/instagram/`.

The same caption works on both.

How each caption is built for the algorithm:
- **Line 1 is the hook.** It's the only line people see before "…more", so it's a challenge, never a spoiler.
- **"Guess before you swipe".** Viewers spend longer on every question slide, and people comment their guess.
- **"Your rank is on the last slide".** This gives people a reason to swipe to slide 12, which raises carousel completion.
- **One easy comment format** ("3/5") keeps the barrier to commenting low.
- **One save or share prompt per post.** Saves and shares weigh heavily.
- **A pinned comment** starts a second conversation under the post.
- **No country names in the captions**, so nothing gets spoiled.

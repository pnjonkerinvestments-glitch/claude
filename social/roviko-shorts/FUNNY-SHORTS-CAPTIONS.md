# Funny shorts 4–10: captions, pinned comments and hashtags

There is one funny short per day for a week, next to the Flag a Day video and the "Guess the country" carousel.
Each one ends on a question for the comments. The caption repeats that question, and the pinned comment kicks off the thread.

**Posting tips**
- **Pin your own comment** straight after posting. A thread that already exists gets more replies.
- **Reply to the first 20 comments in the first hour**, ideally with a short video reply on the funniest wrong answer. Video replies count as new posts and send people back to the original.
- **Use the same caption on TikTok and Instagram.** On Instagram, put the hashtags at the bottom of the caption, 3 to 5 at most.
- **Sound:** post with the video's own sound (it's already in the video). Don't add a trending sound over it.

## Facts checked
- Africa (30.4 million km², including Western Sahara) is about 14 times Greenland (2.17 million km²). On a Mercator map they look about the same size.
- Brasília has been Brazil's capital since 21 April 1960. It was built as a planned city. Before that, the capital was Rio de Janeiro.
- Vienna to Canberra is about 15,900 km on the great circle.
- Astana is the capital of Kazakhstan. Almaty was the capital until 1997.
- Australia's mainland is about 4,000 km from west to east (Steep Point to Cape Byron, about 3,990 km on the great circle). The Moon's diameter is 3,475 km (NASA). The difference is about 500 km.
- The old "Russia is bigger than Pluto" claim is no longer true: with New Horizons' radius of 1,188 km, Pluto's surface is about 17.7 million km², more than Russia's 17.1 million.
- Great Britain is the island with England, Scotland and Wales. The United Kingdom is Great Britain plus Northern Ireland. Ireland is a separate country.
- All names are made up. There are no other brands and no store badges. The only call to action is roviko.app.

- San Marino: 61 km², completely surrounded by Italy. Vatican City: about 0.49 km², inside Rome. The third enclaved country is Lesotho (inside South Africa).
- Look-alike flags: Chad and Romania (Chad's blue is a little darker), Indonesia and Monaco (different proportions), Ireland and Ivory Coast (mirrored), Australia and New Zealand (New Zealand's stars are red, four of them).

- Africa has 54 UN member states.

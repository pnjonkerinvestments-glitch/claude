# Flag a Day: 30 captions (TikTok and Instagram Reels)

One video a day: `out/flag-a-day/flag-a-day-01.mp4` … `-30.mp4` (1080×1920). The same caption works on both platforms.

How each caption is built:
- **Line 1 is the hook**, depending on the level (easy, medium, hard, trap). Never the answer.
- **"Comment before 0":** people comment during the 3 seconds.
- **"Streak":** the series counts days ("Day 7/30"). People come back to keep their streak, and every day they comment ✅ or ❌.
- **Trap days:** the pinned comment asks "Team A or Team B?". The two look-alike flags start a debate in the comments.
- **Video length:** 7 seconds. Short videos get watched to the end and replayed, and both count heavily.

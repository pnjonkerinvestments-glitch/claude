# "Sounds fake, but it's true": 5 fun-fact videos

`out/fun-facts/fun-fact-1.mp4` … `-5.mp4`: 19.6 s each, 1080×1920, 60 fps. Each one is a country fact played as a quiz. The videos are built to entertain; the caption is what sends people to roviko.app.

## How each video hooks viewers and keeps them watching
| Second | What happens | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Frame 1 already shows the hook ("Bet you get this **wrong** 👀"), the question and A/B/C | About 71% of viewers decide in the first 3 seconds. Frame 1 is also the thumbnail, and a question in it makes people stop scrolling |
| 0 | Top pill: "BONUS FACT AT THE END 👀" | An open loop: viewers stay until it pays off. It pulses during the fact so they don't forget it |
| 1–5 | "Comment A, B or C 👇" + a 3-2-1 timer | Interactive: people comment before they know the answer. Comments push the video in the algorithm, and people come back to see if they were right |
| 5 | "Time's up" on the song's break (near silence, ticking) | A pattern break: the sound drops away, so people look up |
| 5–7 | The wrong answers go red one by one, the tempting one last | Suspense: you only find out in the last second |
| 7 | The answer floods green on the song's drop, Roviko: "WHAT?! 🤯" | The payoff, with the strongest beat in the song |
| 8–12 | The fact on a map, with numbers | The "wait, really?" moment, the reason people share and save it |
| 12–15 | **BONUS** (the pill turns green) + a second fact | The open loop pays off, and people watch to the end |
| 15–17 | "Got it right? Comment ✅ or ❌ 👇" | A second reason to comment |
| 17–19.6 | End card: roviko.app | The only call to action in the video. The real push to the site is in the caption and pinned comment |

**Length:** 19.6 s. Quiz videos live on the gap between question and answer. Too short and nobody joins in; too long and people scroll on. 15–30 s is the range for edutainment.

## The caption formula (from video to roviko.app)
1. **Line 1 = the hook again** (only the first line shows in the feed).
2. **Line 2 = a question for the comments** (A, B or C).
3. **Line 3 = the link with a promise:** say what people get, not just "link in bio". For example: "One quiz like this every day at roviko.app (link in bio). No account needed." A call to action works better when it is specific and the destination keeps the caption's promise.
4. **3–5 hashtags.** On Instagram, put them at the bottom.

**Setup (one time):**
- **Bio:** "Geography facts that sound fake 🌍 Play the daily quiz 👇 roviko.app"
- **Link in bio:** straight to roviko.app, not a link page with 10 buttons. Every extra click loses roughly 40–60% of visitors.
- **On TikTok without a bio link** (fewer than 1,000 followers): write "roviko.app" in full, so people can type it.

**Pinned comment:** post it straight after uploading and pin it. It gives the thread its first answer, and it is your second place for roviko.app.

## Posting tips
- **Order:** start with 1 (Brazil) or 4 (Canada), the most surprising ones.
- **Reply to comments in the first hour:** give the person who wrote the funniest wrong answer a reply. Video replies count as new posts and send people back to the original.
- **Series:** "SOUNDS FAKE · #N" builds a series. A follower who wants #6 follows the account.
- **Same caption on both platforms.** On Instagram, the line "link in bio" matters even more, because links in captions aren't clickable there.

## Facts checked
- **France and Brazil:** the Brazil–France border (French Guiana) is 730 km, France's longest land border. The border with Spain is the longest in Europe, but it is shorter.
- **Sudan:** about 200–255 Nubian pyramids (Meroë, Nuri, el-Kurru). Egypt has about 118–138. The Nubian sides are about 70° steep; the Great Pyramid of Giza's are 52°.
- **Saudi Arabia:** it has no permanent rivers, only wadis, and is the largest country without one. It is the world's largest producer of desalinated water.
- **Canada:** HydroLAKES (Messager et al., 2016) counts about 880,000 lakes of 10 ha or more in Canada, 62% of 1.42 million worldwide. Canada has the world's longest coastline.
- **Liechtenstein:** it and Uzbekistan are the only double-landlocked countries. Liechtenstein has no airport; the nearest big one is Zurich.
- **Saint Martin:** the island is split between France (Saint-Martin) and the Netherlands (Sint Maarten, part of the Kingdom of the Netherlands). The island in the bonus of video 1 is drawn as an illustration, not to scale.
- **Map data and flags:** the shapes come from `roviko-carousels/data/geo.js`. French Guiana comes from Natural Earth 1:110m. The flags are the app's own.
